01 — Our manifesto

Intelligence
isn't complete
if people are missing.

AI can be extraordinary and still overlook people. Aggregate performance can conceal meaningful differences in who is served well.

We seek to investigate representation gaps in data, evaluate real differences in model behavior, and explore interventions that can improve reliability across diverse human contexts. Our starting focus is women, with a broader commitment to underserved communities and intersectional experiences.

We focus on testable claims rather than symbolic promises. No universal metric can capture the complexity of human identity; meaningful evaluation requires context, consent, uncertainty, and participation.

Our guiding question: Who is missing from the model — and what evidence would show it?
02 — Proposed research agenda

From invisible gaps
to visible evidence.

Four initial research directions. These are concepts under development — not completed studies or externally validated products.

RESEARCH / 01Proposed
◉

Representation Atlas

Map data coverage and documentation gaps, revealing where important experiences may be absent, sparse, or poorly understood.

RESEARCH / 02Proposed
▦

Evaluation Benchmarks

Design reproducible, context-specific evaluations that report disaggregated performance, uncertainty, and clear limitations.

RESEARCH / 03Proposed
⌁

Model Behavior Audits

Study performance differences through controlled comparisons, robustness analysis, and carefully selected outcome measures.

RESEARCH / 04Proposed
↗

Evidence-Based Interventions

Test whether better data, adaptation, or evaluation methods improve outcomes without creating new harms or trade-offs.

03 — Flagship research concept

Meet the
Representation
Observatory.

An envisioned research framework to discover, evaluate, and address gaps in how AI serves people.

The Observatory is a proposed open research programme, not a currently deployed monitoring platform. It aims to connect documentation, careful measurement, reproducible methods, and community-informed evaluation.

01
Discover

Identify the relevant groups, contexts, and possible coverage gaps.

02
Evaluate

Measure outcomes with meaningful cohorts, uncertainty, and safeguards.

03
Improve

Test remedies and document what works, what fails, and why.

04 — What we stand for

Bold ambition.
Scientific integrity.

Inclusion is a research challenge, not a slogan. Every proposed study must remain answerable to the evidence.

✳

Evidence before claims

Test hypotheses, report uncertainty, and avoid overstating results.

✳

Privacy by design

Respect consent, context, and data minimization at every stage.

✳

Intersectional thinking

Recognize that experiences cannot be reduced to single labels.

✳

Reproducible science

Favor transparent metrics, protocols, and documented limitations.

✳

Participation matters

Create meaningful roles for communities affected by the systems.

✳

Independent inquiry

Maintain clarity about the initiative's stage and actual affiliations.

05 — Community vision

Women-led.
Humanity-wide.

We envision a global community connecting research, engineering, leadership, and human experience. Participation formats are being explored.

01 / CONNECT

02 / BUILD

03 / AMPLIFY
